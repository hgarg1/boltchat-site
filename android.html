You're in, friend

Two quick steps, both with the Google account you use on Google Play:

  1. Join the testers group. Join the group Pick the Google account you use on your phone and request to join; it takes effect when Google adds you.
  2. Become a tester. Open the tester link on your Android phone, tap Become a tester, then install Boltchat from Google Play.

We also have your email so we can tell you about updates. Bring your own Groq key: get a free one.

📱 What you need

Android 8.0 or later, a Google account for Google Play, and your own Groq API key (a free tier is available). Your key is stored encrypted in the Android Keystore.

🧪 It's a beta

It works and it's the same engine as the Windows app, but expect rough edges. Use Report under a reply, or write to us, if something's off.

🔒 Same promise

No account, no ads, no tracking. Chats stay on your phone; requests go straight to Groq with your key.

What's in the beta

Everything from the PC, made for a phone

⚡

Streaming that survives the back button

Replies keep arriving when you switch apps. A short foreground service holds the reply and notifies you when it lands.

🌐

/web and /code

Groq's hosted web search and Python on GPT-OSS 120B and 20B. You see the search URLs and the code, not a black box.

∑

Math, markdown, code

Tables, fenced code with syntax colour, and LaTeX typeset like a textbook.

🎙

Voice, then a draft

Tap the mic and talk. Whisper transcribes into the composer so you can fix a name before you send.

🗂

Projects

Group chats, add shared instructions and files (PDF, Word, Excel, EPUB, code and more). Search understands meaning and runs on your phone, and every reply shows what the project contributed.

📰

Ideas from seven newsrooms

The start screen suggests chats from your memories and today's stories, merged from BBC, NPR, PBS, CSM, DW, France 24 and CBC.

✦

Optional memory

Off until you turn it on. Review, edit and delete everything it remembers.

⇄

Bring your chats

Export a backup, or import the Windows app's conversations.json and memories to continue on your phone.

🔒

Private by design

Your key is encrypted in the Android Keystore. Chats, memories and project files never go to cloud backup.
