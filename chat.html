Boltchat in your browser

Nothing to install. Your chats and your Groq key stay in this browser, and messages go straight to Groq. How it handles your data

Open the chat full screen ↗

Stays in this browser

Chats, memories, projects and their files are stored only in this browser. There is no Boltchat account and no Boltchat server.

Straight to Groq

Bring your own Groq API key (a free tier is available). Requests go directly from this page to Groq and nowhere else.

Back up or erase

Settings → About has Download backup, Restore and Erase. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything too.

Want the Code workspace?

The coding agent, terminal and built-in browser need a real PC. They are in the Windows app.
